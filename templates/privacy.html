Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information.

Data We Collect

Spendly collects the account details you provide, such as your name and email address, along with the expense records you choose to enter. We may also receive basic technical information needed to keep the service secure and working properly.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and improve expense tracking, maintain your account, respond to support requests, and protect Spendly from misuse. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored so you can access your records across sessions. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted service providers for hosting, authentication, analytics, or other infrastructure. These providers may process information only as needed to deliver their services and are expected to handle it responsibly.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to ask about your personal information, please contact the Spendly team through the support channel provided with your account.
